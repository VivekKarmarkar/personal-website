The Oldest Diagnostic, Reimagined

For thousands of years, physicians have detected tumors through palpation—the simple act of pressing and feeling. Cancerous tissue is typically 5-20× stiffer than healthy tissue. Your doctor knows this. Now, so does our AI.

But here's the challenge: when you press on the surface, you only feel the surface. The tumor is hidden inside. How do you reconstruct what you cannot directly see?

"This is not just an engineering problem—it's an inverse problem, one of the most beautiful and challenging classes of problems in mathematics. We're asking: given the effect (surface deformation), what was the cause (interior stiffness distribution)?"

PAT Scan solves this by combining two powerful ideas: the physics of how materials deform (Finite Element Method) with the pattern-recognition power of deep learning (U-Net). The result? An AI that learns to "see" through touch.
